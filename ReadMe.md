## 💫 About Me

I am a Data Engineer and Computer Science graduate passionate about building scalable, high-throughput distributed data platforms and automated ETL workflows. 

- 🔭 **Current Focus**: Architecting enterprise Medallion Lakehouse pipelines (Bronze, Silver, Gold), stream/batch data ingestion, and building fault-tolerant workflow orchestrators.
- ⚡ **Author & Maintainer**: [**The Data Engineering Book**](https://github.com/swapnanilchatterjee/Data-Engineering-Book) — an open-source, hands-on production curriculum and 10-day PySpark masterclass.
- 🛠️ **Core Strengths**: Apache Spark internals & cluster performance tuning, recursive CTEs & window analytics in PostgreSQL, and dimensional star-schema modeling in Power BI.
- 🏆 **Community & Leadership**: 2x Hackathon Finalist, former PR & Outreach Lead for GFG SNU, and Technical Content Writer with Coding Ninjas SNU.
- 📫 **Let's Connect**: Open to exciting opportunities in Data Engineering, Analytics Engineering, and Big Data platform development.
